## Overall Assessment

### Strong Areas

The Ed25519 and secp256k1 Go compatibility coverage is **excellent**. Every component has been verified with hardcoded Go test vectors and byte-equality checks. The ECIES and AES-GCM paths are similarly well-covered with complete end-to-end test vectors.

### Weak Areas

1. **Searchable encryption** has a confirmed HIGH severity bug (Finding 10) masked by circular test validation (Finding 11)
2. **secp256r1** has systematically weaker test coverage than the other key types (Finding 13), with a known S-normalization divergence (Finding 04)
3. **JWT** is untested for cross-implementation compatibility (Finding 12)

### 1.0 Blockers Identified

| Issue | Status |
|---|---|
| SE tag UTF-8 lossy divergence (Finding 10) | **MUST FIX** before 1.0 |
| secp256r1 S-normalization CID divergence (Finding 04) | Investigate: does secp256r1 appear in IPLD blocks? |
